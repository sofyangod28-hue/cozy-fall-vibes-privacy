Cozy Fall Vibes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Cozy Fall Vibes is a wallpaper app published by READY COM. This policy describes the information handled by the app and its advertising partners.

  • Android package: com.cozyfall.vibesautumn
  • Privacy contact: kolonil97@outlook.com

App features

Wallpapers are included in the app and can be browsed offline. The app does not require an account. Setting or sharing an image creates a temporary local copy on your device. The app does not upload wallpaper images to a READY COM server. When you share an image, the app or service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location. If you email the developer, your email address and message are used to respond to your request.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network adapters. Depending on availability and the mediation settings, these providers may receive ad requests. The app currently shows banner ads in wallpaper lists and requests an interstitial after every third wallpaper image is opened, when an ad is available. Ad delivery requires an internet connection. AdMob bidding is not currently integrated in this app.

On first launch, the app asks whether you agree to personalized ad processing. Choosing “Agree” or “No thanks” is passed to LevelPlay as the GDPR consent value for Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. The app does not currently use Google’s UMP consent SDK or provide an in-app control to change this choice later. Ads may still be requested after either choice; the choice is passed to the networks to apply their settings and does not guarantee that an ad will be personalized, shown, or suppressed. Consent requirements and available choices can depend on your location and applicable law.

Advertising providers may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and operating system details, network and app interaction data, ad views and clicks, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention data. The exact information depends on the provider, configuration, device, and applicable settings. Providers use information under their own policies to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate their services. They may share information with their service providers and advertising partners.

Provider policies:

  • Unity / LevelPlay / ironSource
  • Meta

Permissions, storage, and choices

The app requests internet access for advertising, Android advertising ID access for advertising SDKs, and permission to set your wallpaper. Temporary wallpaper copies are stored in app-specific temporary storage and may be removed by the app, Android, or you. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data but does not delete information already held by advertising providers.

You can manage or reset the advertising ID and available ad privacy settings in Android settings. You can also use the provider privacy controls linked above. These controls may not stop all ad requests or data processing. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights over personal information. Contact the developer at kolonil97@outlook.com for requests about information held by READY COM; requests for information held by an ad provider may need to be sent to that provider.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided information to the developer, contact the privacy email above.

Security and changes

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
